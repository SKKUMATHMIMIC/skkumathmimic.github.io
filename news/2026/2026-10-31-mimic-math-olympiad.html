Open to all SKKU undergraduates, the competition brought together 12 students from a range of departments, including mathematics, chemical engineering, materials science and engineering, and electrical and electronic engineering. The MMO consisted of seven proof-based problems to be solved in 150 minutes. The problems were designed so that they could be solved with only the basic mathematics covered in BSM (Basic Science and Mathematics) courses, but they demanded rigorous reasoning and creative ideas rather than routine computation. A dedicated problem-setting and review team prepared the problems, and Professor Jang Soo Kim and Professor Seungwon Kim of the SKKU Department of Mathematics also took part in reviewing them. After grading, a total of nine students received awards. The winners are as follows (entry year, department): - **Gold Award**: Junho Jung ('24, School of Chemical Engineering) - **Silver Award**: Hyunwoo Kim ('24, Department of Mathematics), Imtaek Lee ('22, Department of Materials Science and Engineering), Hyeoncheol Jung ('26, Liberal Studies) - **Bronze Award**: Junwan Park ('26, School of Electronic and Electrical Engineering), Insung Baek ('21, Department of Mathematics), Hanrim Song ('21, Department of Mathematics), Hyunju Oh ('23, School of Electronic and Electrical Engineering), Jeongmyeong Hong ('21, School of Chemical Engineering) With the support of the SKKU Department of Mathematics and the president of the alumni association, the Gold, Silver, and Bronze Award winners received AirPods (5th generation), a Logitech keyboard and mouse set, and a power bank, respectively.
