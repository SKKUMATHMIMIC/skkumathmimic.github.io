The authors are Hee-Sung Ahn, Hee-Jae Kim, and Min-Guk Kim (SKKU undergraduates), Seung-Jin Lee (Yonsei University graduate program), and Dr. Hong-Taek Jung of KIAS. The paper's main result shows that, for a closed orientable surface of genus $g > 1$ and any $\epsilon > 0$, whenever $n > 2$ the $\epsilon$-thick part of the $\mathrm{PSL}_n(\mathbb{R})$-Hitchin-Riemann moduli space has infinite Atiyah–Bott–Goldman volume. This stands in contrast to Mumford's compactness criterion for the classical moduli space, showing that no analogous property holds for the $\mathrm{PSL}_n(\mathbb{R})$-Hitchin component, the higher-rank generalization of Teichmüller space. In other words, it shows that the thick part of the Hitchin-Riemann moduli space is not compact. This research was an R&E project independently planned within MIMIC and carried out under the guidance of Dr. Hong-Taek Jung of KIAS. Undergraduate students spent a full year working through the challenging subject of higher Teichmüller theory, and the result became an arXiv preprint. Among the authors, Min-Guk Kim and Hee-Sung Ahn remain active members of MIMIC today.
