Furthermore, given that many deep learning methodologies are grounded in statistical ideas, statistics also plays a vital role in understanding AI. Among statistical approaches, nonparametric statistics is closely connected with various machine learning methods in that it performs inference and prediction without making strong assumptions about the specific form of the data distribution. In this talk, we aim to examine widely known methods—such as kNN, Decision Trees, and Gaussian Mixture Models—not merely from an algorithmic or deep learning perspective, but from the viewpoint of statistical theory. We will introduce the foundational ideas behind each method, why they work effectively, and how model complexity and tuning parameters are selected and optimized. In particular, rather than presupposing that data follows a specific parametric distribution, the nonparametric perspective estimates the shape of distributions or functions directly from observed data to conduct statistical inference.
