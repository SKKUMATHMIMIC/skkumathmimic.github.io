In financial markets in particular, investors must determine their investment strategies dynamically while accounting for the random fluctuations of asset prices, and such problems can be formulated using stochastic differential equations (SDEs). In this seminar, we introduce the basic concepts of stochastic optimal control through the Merton portfolio problem. We first briefly review stochastic differential equations and Itô's formula, and then explain the value function and the dynamic programming principle (DPP).
