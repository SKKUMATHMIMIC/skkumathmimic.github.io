Prior to this work it was known only for one row, one column, and two columns. We solve the two-row case λ = (n, n): the coefficient of sμ in ch(Lλ) counts standard Young tableaux of shape μ satisfying a block-major-index condition, refined by a spin parity inherited from Yamanouchi domino tableaux via Carré–Leclerc and van Leeuwen.
