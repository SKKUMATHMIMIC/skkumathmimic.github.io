Then, can one still make a profit in such a game with a well-designed betting strategy? The martingale betting strategy, which doubles the bet after every loss, seems to guarantee a profit, since one is sure to come out ahead as long as one eventually wins. Can this really happen? Starting from this question, this talk introduces the martingale, one of the key concepts in the theory of stochastic processes. Based on random walks and conditional expectation, we will understand the mathematical meaning of martingales and examine how betting strategies and the stopping time of a game affect the expected return.
