However, there are also polyhedra with holes for which this quantity is not 2. We call the quantity vertices - edges + faces the Euler characteristic. Surprisingly, if two shapes (spaces) are homeomorphic, then they have the same Euler characteristic. Conversely, if two shapes have different Euler characteristics, they cannot be homeomorphic. In other words, by counting the vertices, edges, and faces of shapes, you can prove that a mug and a soccer ball are not homeomorphic. Ohhori… Isn't that fascinating?
